# Integrated Pest & Disease Management (IPM) Guide

## 1. Early Pest Scouting & Monitoring
- Install yellow sticky traps (10-15 per acre) for sucking pests (whiteflies, aphids, thrips).
- Deploy pheromone traps for Helicoverpa armigera (bollworm/fruit borer) and Spodoptera litura (armyworm) at 4 traps per acre for early detection.
- Monitor economic threshold levels (ETL) before deploying chemical interventions.

## 2. Common Crop Symptoms and Remedies
- **Tomato Yellow Leaf Curl Virus (TYLCV)**: Transmitted by whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*). Symptoms: upward curling, yellowing leaf margins, stunted bushy growth. Control whitefly vectors using Neem seed kernel extract (NSKE 5%) or Imidacloprid (0.3 ml/L). Remove severely infected plants.
- **Early Blight (*Alternaria solani*)**: Concentric ring 'target board' dark brown spots on older foliage. Apply Mancozeb 75% WP (2 g/L) or Chlorothalonil at first sign of lesions. Avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation.
- **Late Blight (*Phytophthora infestans*)**: Water-soaked lesions turning black during cool, humid spells. Apply systemic fungicides like Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (2.5 g/L).
- **Rice Blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae*)**: Spindle-shaped lesions with grayish centers. Maintain balanced nitrogen application; avoid excess urea. Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP at 0.6 g/L.

## 3. Biological and Cultural Controls
- Encourage natural predators: Ladybird beetles, Chrysoperla, predatory mites.
- Crop rotation with non-host legumes breaks pest and pathogen life cycles.
- Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens seed treatment (10 g/kg seed) prevents seedling damping-off and root rots.
