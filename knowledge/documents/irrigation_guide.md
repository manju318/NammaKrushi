# Smart Irrigation Scheduling & Water Management

## 1. Irrigation Methodologies
- **Drip Irrigation**: Delivers water and water-soluble fertilizers (fertigation) directly to the crop root zone at low pressures (1.0 to 1.5 kg/cm²). Saves 40-60% water, increases yield by 20-30%, and drastically reduces weed propagation.
- **Micro-Sprinklers**: Ideal for closely spaced vegetables, pulses, and nurseries. Provides microclimate cooling during peak summer heat waves.
- **Furrow / Basin Irrigation**: Requires precise land leveling to prevent waterlogging and root asphyxiation.

## 2. Weather-Based Irrigation Adjustments
- **Evapotranspiration (ET0) Rule**: If daily reference evapotranspiration is 5 mm and crop coefficient (Kc) is 0.8, daily crop water requirement is 4 mm (40,000 liters/hectare).
- **Rain Forecast Precautions**: When precipitation probability exceeds 65% within 24-48 hours, postpone irrigation to avoid nutrient leaching and root suffocation.
- **Critical Growth Stages**: Moisture stress during flowering, pollination, and grain filling stages severely penalizes total yield. Prioritize irrigation during these phases.
