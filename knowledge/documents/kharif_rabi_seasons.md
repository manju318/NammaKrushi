# Cropping Seasons & Crop Calendars in Indian Agriculture

## 1. Kharif Season (Monsoon: June to October)
- **Climatic Characteristics**: Warm, humid, dependent on South-West monsoon rainfall.
- **Key Crops**: Paddy (Rice), Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Groundnut, Pigeonpea (Tur), Green Gram (Moong).
- **Critical Practices**: Pre-monsoon land preparation, seed bed treatment, proper field drainage channels to prevent waterlogging during flash downpours.

## 2. Rabi Season (Winter: October to March)
- **Climatic Characteristics**: Cool temperatures, dry spells, relies on residual soil moisture, winter rains (Western Disturbances), and assured irrigation.
- **Key Crops**: Wheat, Chickpea (Gram), Mustard, Barley, Peas, Potato, Onion.
- **Critical Practices**: Timely sowing in late October to early November to evade terminal heat stress during grain filling in February-March.

## 3. Zaid / Summer Season (March to June)
- **Climatic Characteristics**: High ambient temperatures, long sunshine hours, low relative humidity.
- **Key Crops**: Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Okra, Fodder Sorghum.
- **Critical Practices**: Frequent light irrigations (prefer drip), organic mulching to suppress soil evaporation.
